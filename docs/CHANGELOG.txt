2026-09-23  -  03:04 AM - 03:20 AM

Printer presets and paper settings, per request.

Research findings (sources in src/lenticular/core/presets.mjs):
- Epson drivers take image data at 720 ppi (360 in lower modes) and
  resample anything else. So every Epson preset builds at 720 DPI, not the
  app's generic 600, which would have been resampled and blurred.
- Paper matters: glossy resin-coated photo paper keeps thin strips sharp;
  plain paper wicks ink (ghosting) and cockles, which shifts the pitch.
  Thickness does not affect the optics.

Added:
- core/presets.mjs: 10 Epson EcoTank printer presets (ET-2400, 2800, 2850,
  3850, 4800, 4850, 5850, 8500, 8550, 15000) and 13 paper presets (Epson,
  Canon, HP home glossy/semi-gloss/matte + plain), published weight/
  thickness only (blank where not found), lenticular suitability,
  Epson driver paper-type mapping.
- ui/pickers.mjs: grouped printer/paper selects (saved profiles + presets)
  in Calibrate and Create; paper profiles (lw.papers) with "start from
  preset" in the Printer Profiles tab, plus reference tables with sources.
- Lens calibrations record the paper; Create warns on native-DPI mismatch,
  plain paper, and paper differing from the calibration paper; the
  checklist shows the paper and the Epson driver paper-type setting.
- tests/presets.test.mjs (6 tests; 27 total pass).

Verified:
- Browser on :8430: preset selection sets 720 DPI; warnings fire for
  600 DPI and plain paper; Create checklist shows paper + driver setting;
  a preset-based printer profile saves with its native DPI list; both
  reference tables render; no console errors. Test data cleared afterwards.
